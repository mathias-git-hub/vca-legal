VCA (Viral Clip Agent)

VCA (Viral Clip Agent) is a private tool operated by Mathias Løve that prepares short video clips and schedules posts to the operator's own social media accounts.

Use of Google Calendar

VCA uses Google Calendar only to create and manage one calendar that it creates itself, called "VCA posting". In that calendar it places reminders for scheduled posts and reads back changes the operator makes to those reminders. It does not access any other calendars.
